## Objective

Enabled accounts are disabled after 35 days without activity, unless a documented exception applies.

## Population

All 19 local, service, and break-glass accounts on the portal VMs and database (E-01). Employee
accounts are out of scope: they come from the Enterprise Identity Service and are removed when HR
records a separation.

**Reliability:** Kiran Patel ran the listing query on 2026-09-30 with me watching. I checked
the account count against the CMDB service account register (19 = 19).

## Procedure performed

1. Kept the 18 enabled accounts. `adm-pilot-test` was already disabled.
2. Calculated days since last activity as of 2026-09-30.
3. Flagged accounts idle more than 35 days, and checked any exception reference against the
   exception register.
